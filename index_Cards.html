STUDIO APPOSITE

Architectural Design + Research

Studio Apposite is an architectural design and research practice based in Victoria, British Columbia. The studio operates across two parallel bodies of work: a client-facing architectural practice focused on residential renovation and alteration across a range of scales, and an independent research and publishing program dedicated to the study of regional architectural history and theory.

Practice

Studio Apposite works primarily with existing buildings. The studio undertakes renovation, alteration, and small additions through careful measurement, drawing, and construction study, developing new work in close relation to what is already there. Projects are shaped by the specific conditions of the building and site, with an emphasis on proportion, material continuity, and buildability.

Research

Studio Apposite maintains an independent research and publishing program focused on the history and theory of architecture in Victoria and the surrounding region. This work develops through archival research, writing, and image-based study, and is disseminated through publications, exhibitions, and public projects under the imprint Apposite Press. The research program operates as a sustained scholarly practice, contributing to the documentation and interpretation of regional architectural culture.

People

Brandon Poole is an architectural designer, researcher, and educator based in Victoria. He is a PhD Candidate in Architecture at McGill University, where he is a Vadasz Scholar and SSHRC Doctoral Fellow. He holds a Master of Visual Studies from the University of Toronto’s Faculty of Architecture, Landscape, and Design, and a Bachelor of Fine Arts from the University of Victoria. He teaches in the Visual Arts Department at the University of Victoria.

Patrick Rollins is a designer, researcher, and curator. His work has included contributions to From the Ground, Into the Light: Organic Architecture of the Islands and Centennial Square: A Design History at the Wentworth Villa Architectural Museum. Current projects include research and exhibitions focused on regional architectural history and a forthcoming publication on the work of Alan Hodgson.
