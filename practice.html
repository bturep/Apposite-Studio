Architectural Design + Research

Scope

Studio Apposite works primarily with existing houses—renovations, small additions, and precise upgrades where new work must integrate cleanly with what’s already there. The aim is not novelty, but fit: work that is buildable, legible, and continuous with the spatial and material logic of the house.

Services

Renovations + alterations

  • Kitchens and bathrooms
  • Entries, stairs, and circulation fixes
  • Built-ins, millwork, and storage systems
  • Envelope / upgrade strategies (targeted, not wholesale)

Small additions

  • Siting + massing studies that respect the existing house
  • Integration details: rooflines, junctions, proportion, exterior continuity

Measured survey + as-builts

  • Measured drawings (plans / key elevations / sections as needed)
  • Constraint confirmation: head heights, openings, structure “as found”

Approvals + coordination

  • Permit pathway support (scope depends on jurisdiction + existing conditions)
  • Consultant coordination when required (structural / envelope / energy)
  • Contractor collaboration and revision cycles

Deliverables

Deliverables are calibrated to the moment in the project—enough resolution to make decisions early, and the right kind of drawings to build later.

Early-phase

  • Measured base plan + existing conditions diagram
  • Two to three option sets (plans + key elevations / sections)
  • Material and junction precedent notes where helpful

Documentation-phase

  • Permit / construction drawings (scope-specific)
  • Key details and integration sections
  • Revision rounds coordinated with consultant input (as required)

Process

A practical rhythm

Most work moves through a simple sequence: initial call → site visit / measurement → options → refinement → documentation. The emphasis is on clarity: drawings that communicate decisions, constraints, and build logic.

Working with existing houses

Existing conditions are treated as material and evidence, not as inconvenience. We prefer careful integration: what stays, what changes, what must be repaired, and where new work should clearly declare itself.

Fees

Depending on scope, services are structured as fixed-fee phases (concept / schematic, documentation), with hourly support where the work is inherently uncertain (coordination, revisions, agency feedback loops). When a pathway is unclear at the outset, we’ll define a small first phase to establish constraints and confirm next steps before committing further.

If you email a short description of your project, we can propose an appropriate first phase and fee structure.
