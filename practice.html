Practice

Studio Apposite offers architectural design + documentation services for work on existing houses, small additions, and targeted upgrades. The practice emphasizes careful measurement, proportion, and construction logic—work that is buildable, legible, and continuous with what already exists.

Services

Design + documentation

  • Renovations and alterations (kitchens, bathrooms, stairs, entries, built-ins, millwork).
  • Small additions + integration studies (form, proportion, envelope logic, siting).
  • Concept design and options: clear drawings that support decision-making early.
  • Construction / permit drawing sets (as appropriate to scope and jurisdiction).

Existing conditions + as-builts

  • Measured surveys and as-built drawings (plans / elevations / key sections as required).
  • Targeted verification of constraints (structure, head heights, egress conditions, etc.).

Approvals + coordination

  • Permit pathway support: assembling the right scope of drawings and information for review.
  • Code- and bylaw-aware layout checks (egress, ceiling heights, fire separations, etc.) at a “what’s required here?” level.
  • Consultant coordination where needed (structural / energy / envelope / contractor collaboration).
  • Site reviews at key moments to keep intent and buildability aligned.

Special case: suite work

  • Suite legalization / secondary suite drawing packages (scope depends on existing conditions and municipality).
