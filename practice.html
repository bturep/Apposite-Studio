Services

Studio Apposite works primarily with existing houses on Vancouver Island and the Gulf Islands, focusing on design-sensitive repair, alteration, and integration rather than replacement.

Typical work includes

  • Entries and stairs
  • Built-ins and custom millwork
  • Kitchens and bathrooms
  • Decks and exterior interventions
  • Envelope and upgrade strategies (targeted, pragmatic)
  • Renovations and small additions
Approach: careful measurement, proportion, construction logic, and continuity with what already exists.

Deliverables

Deliverables are calibrated to the project phase—enough resolution to make decisions early, and the right drawings to coordinate and build later.

Often includes

  • Measured survey / existing conditions drawings (scope-specific)
  • Options (plans + key elevations / sections)
  • Refined design set for decision-making
  • Documentation for permits / construction (as required)
  • Key junction details where integration matters most

Process

Most projects move through a simple rhythm: initial call → site visit / measurement → options → refinement → documentation.

Working with existing houses

Existing conditions are treated as material and evidence. The aim is clarity: what stays, what changes, what must be repaired, and where new work should declare itself.

Good fit: projects where the “hard part” is integration—junctions, continuity, proportion, and legibility.
