STUDIO APPOSITE

Practice

Studio Apposite works with existing residential and commercial buildings, undertaking renovations, alterations, small additions and tenancy improvements. Projects typically begin with a careful review of constraints—site, bylaw, and construction—so that scope and approvals are understood early. Design develops through measured drawings and iterative studies, resulting in precise documentation that supports permitting and construction.
