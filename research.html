Research

Studio Apposite operates as a hybrid research platform: archival surveys, interviews, and image-based studies that culminate in small publications and occasional exhibitions.

Working themes

Regional architectural history · building as evidence · documentation as design · repair and continuity · the archive as a practical tool.

If you have material we should know about—photographs, drawings, correspondence, or leads—email us.

Publication

Photo-book series: West Coast Modern / regional practices

A small-format publication program focused on discrete buildings and site conditions—prioritizing clear image sequences, concise captions, and archival trace materials.

Status: in development

Format

Short runs, high legibility. Each issue assembles a record: photographs, drawing fragments, construction logic, and commentary—enough to be useful, not encyclopedic.

Outputs: printed booklet · PDF edition

Exhibition

Archival studies + image-based installations

Occasional exhibitions that treat documentation as material: photographs, scans, and re-assembled evidence staged as a spatial argument.

Status: occasional / by project

Purpose

To build a local record of buildings and practices—and to carry that material back into client work, where it becomes reference, constraint, and grounding.

Outputs: wall text · prints · small publication

Archival survey

Fieldwork + interviews

Site visits, measured observations, and conversations that identify what exists, what has changed, and what survives in drawings, photographs, and memory.

Status: ongoing

How it shows up in design

Better briefs, better constraints, better proportion decisions. Research becomes a practical tool: a way to make interventions that fit what’s already there.

Outputs: notes · image sets · as-built fragments
