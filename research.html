STUDIO APPOSITE

Research

Studio Apposite undertakes focused research on domestic space and residential form, concentrating on postwar modernism and its extensions into the 1990s. The work draws on primary sources, interviews, site visits, and documentary practices, producing sustained studies and working documents that address an under-studied period of regional domestic architecture.

Publications

Mayhew Studio

Publication — regional architectural and studio history (book project).

Publication
Victoria, BC
2026

Mayhew Studio is the inaugural volume in a book series dedicated to domestic and house-scale architectures in the Capital Regional District. The first volume centres on the collaboration between sculptor Elza Mayhew and architect Alan Hodgson in the design and construction of Mayhew’s sculpture studio at 330 St. Lawrence Street (1969). Conceived as both workplace and dwelling, the studio is read as a confluence of two practices and sensibilities, where architectural space and sculptural thinking intersect.

The publication brings together newly digitized archival material from Alan Hodgson’s personal slide collection, held at UVic Special Collections, alongside film stills drawn from Karl Spreitz’s documentary footage of Mayhew and her studio, with an introduction by Patrick Rollins and an essay by artist Lynda Gammon.

Published under the imprint Apposite Press, the book establishes the series’ methodological and graphic approach: compact, image-driven volumes that treat individual houses as sites of intensified architectural thought. Rather than positioning Victoria’s architectural history in opposition to dominant regional narratives, the series advances a quieter, more affective mode of criticism—one attentive to modest scale, material specificity, and the cultural life of buildings.

Status: In development. Spring 2026 Release.
