Studio Apposite

Architecture + Research

Design-sensitive work at any scale.

Studio Apposite takes on small projects (entries, decks, bathrooms) and larger renovations or additions, with particular attention to repair and alteration that integrates carefully with what exists.

Practice

We work with existing buildings as material, evidence, and constraint. The goal is not novelty. The goal is fit: proportion, continuity, and buildability—decisions that hold up over time.

Typical work includes entries and stairs, built-ins, kitchens and bathrooms, decks, envelope and upgrade strategies, and renovations or additions.

Expanded Research

Alongside client work, Studio Apposite is developing a small publication series focused on specific buildings and practices. Each issue assembles drawings, photographs, and archival research into a compact object.
