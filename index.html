STUDIO APPOSITE

Architectural Design + Research

Studio Apposite is an architectural design and research practice working primarily with existing houses on Vancouver Island and the Gulf Islands. The studio specializes in residential renovations, small additions, heritage-sensitive upgrades, and custom millwork—balancing craft and composition toward design that is apposite: fitting to situation and context.

Alongside client work, Studio Apposite operates as a hybrid research platform, producing exhibitions, publications, and archival studies focused on regional architectural history. This research directly informs the design practice, grounding each project in the spatial, material, and cultural conditions of its site.

Practice

Studio Apposite works primarily with existing buildings, treating them as material, evidence, and constraint.

Typical work includes entries and stairs, built-ins, kitchens and bathrooms, decks, envelope upgrades, and renovations or additions.
