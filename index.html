Studio Apposite

Architecture + Research — Design-sensitive work at any scale.

Studio Apposite is the architectural design and research practice of Brandon Poole and Patrick Rollins. The studio specializes in residential renovations, small additions, heritage-sensitive upgrades, and custom millwork, balancing craft and composition toward design that is apposite: fitting to situation and context.

Alongside client work, Studio Apposite operates as a hybrid research platform, producing exhibitions, publications, and archival studies focused on the architecture of Vancouver Island and the Gulf Islands. This research directly informs the design practice, grounding each project in the spatial, material, and cultural conditions of its site.

Practice

Studio Apposite works primarily with existing buildings, treating them as material, evidence, and constraint. Projects are approached through careful measurement, proportion, and construction logic, with particular attention to repair, alteration, and integration rather than replacement. The aim is not novelty, but fit: work that is buildable, legible, and continuous with what already exists.

People

Brandon Poole is a PhD Candidate at the McGill School of Architecture, where he is a Vadasz Scholar and SSHRC Doctoral Fellow. He holds a Master of Visual Studies (MVS) from the University of Toronto’s Faculty of Architecture, Landscape, and Design, and a Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA, Honours with Distinction) from the University of Victoria. Alongside architectural design, he is an artist, a filmmaker, and a lecturer in the Visual Arts Department at UVic.

Patrick Rollins is a designer, researcher, and curator. Recent projects include contributions to From the Ground, Into the Light: Organic Architecture of the Islands and Centennial Square: A Design History, both at the Wentworth Villa Architectural Museum. Upcoming projects include exhibitions on the furniture of architect and architectural historian Peter Neve Cotton, and a photo-book series beginning with Alan Hodgson’s Elza Mayhew Studio (1969).
