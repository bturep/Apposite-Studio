STUDIO APPOSITE

Architectural Design + Research

Studio Apposite is the architectural design and research practice of Brandon Poole and Patrick Rollins. The studio specializes in residential renovations, small additions, heritage-sensitive upgrades, and custom millwork, balancing craft and composition toward design that is apposite: fitting to situation and context.

Alongside client work, Studio Apposite operates as a hybrid research platform, producing exhibitions, publications, and archival studies focused on the architecture of Vancouver Island and the Gulf Islands.

Practice

Studio Apposite works primarily with existing buildings, treating them as material, evidence, and constraint.

Typical work includes entries and stairs, built-ins, kitchens and bathrooms, decks, envelope strategies, and renovations or additions.
